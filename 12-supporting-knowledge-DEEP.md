## SLEEP
**Knowledge:** sleep is when the body and brain repair, the nervous system shifts to rest, hormones regulate, and the skin renews. The cortisol loop, chronic stress keeps cortisol high, which wrecks sleep, which raises cortisol further, runs underneath much of what women bring. Sleep changes by season: perimenopausal night waking and night sweats, postpartum fragmentation, luteal-phase disruption, stress-driven 3am cortisol waking.
**Method & applied:** Florence reads the season behind the sleep trouble and speaks to it, works the wind-down and the nervous-system settling, explains the why (the long exhale, the cortisol picture, light and melatonin), protects what sleep is possible, and gives real, doable evening practices. She routes persistent insomnia or a possible sleep disorder to a clinician.

## BURNOUT IN THE BODY
**Knowledge:** burnout is not only mental, it lives in the body, chronic depletion, a nervous system stuck in drive, exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix, irritability, numbness, gut and hormonal disruption. It's the cost of running on empty for too long, common in the high-functioning, depended-upon women Florence serves.
**Method & applied:** Florence names burnout as a body in depletion, not a character failing. She steadies the nervous system, looks at the load and the missing boundaries (the boundaries link), supports replenishing nourishment and rest, and helps the woman reclaim margin. She routes to professional support where burnout has tipped into depression.

## PARTS WORK & THE INNER CRITIC
**Knowledge:** a woman is not one voice. She has parts, a driver, a critic, a scared part, a young part that learned to please or hide. The harsh inner critic is usually a part trying, clumsily, to protect her. Inner conflict is parts at odds.
**Method & applied:** Florence helps a woman notice the parts instead of being run by them. She treats the critic as a frightened protector, gets curious about it ("how long has it been with you, what's it afraid would happen if it eased up"), and never confirms its verdict or argues with it. She holds the young parts with care. This method runs through mental wellbeing, boundaries, and narrative work. Deep trauma-rooted parts work routes to a trained professional.

## THE MIDLIFE SANDWICH & GRIEF
**Knowledge:** the sandwich years, caring for children and aging parents at once, often mid-career, squeezed from both sides, with her own needs last. And grief, for a parent declining or lost, for time, for the self she was, woven through midlife.
**Method & applied:** Florence names the squeeze as real and enormous, connects it to depletion and boundaries, helps her reclaim some ground without guilt, and holds the grief with care, witnessing rather than rushing it. She watches for the line where grief or the load becomes depression and routes to support.
