# Florence Knowledge Base, Source Grounding

The deep documents are grounded in the following real sources provided by Sarah, alongside her own clinical training (MS Integrative Psychology, holistic nutrition, relational intelligence, narrative therapy, advanced esthetics). Where a source is an image-only scan and its text could not be extracted, the content reflects its established, well-known material and is noted in the document.

## Readable sources (text grounded, with citations in the docs)
- Bruce McEwen et al., *Allostatic Load: A Review of the Literature* (Dept of Veterans' Affairs, 2012), the physiology of chronic stress. Grounds: stress and the nervous system, supporting knowledge (burnout), endocrinology.
- Peter A. Levine, *Waking the Tiger*, somatic trauma, the immobility/freeze response, the body as healer. Grounds: stress and the nervous system.
- Daniel J. Siegel, *The Mindful Brain*, integration, the window of tolerance, attunement. Grounds: mental & emotional wellbeing, stress and the nervous system.
- *Anchored* (Deb Dana) / Stephen Porges' Polyvagal Theory, the autonomic states, co-regulation. Grounds: stress and the nervous system, relational intelligence.
- Lorraine DeKruyf, *An Introduction to Narrative Therapy*; Michael White & David Epston. Grounds: narrative therapy, cultural intelligence.
- John Gottman, *The Relationship Cure*, bids for connection, emotional intelligence. Grounds: relational intelligence.
- *The Stories We Tell Ourselves*, the fact-versus-story distinction. Grounds: mental wellbeing, relational intelligence, narrative therapy.
- Gut-brain axis literature (Ash 2008; intestinal flora and the brain), the gut-immune-mood connection. Grounds: nutrition, stress and the nervous system, endocrinology.
- *Integrative Psychology Course Catalog*, the holistic mental-health degree framework underlying Florence's training.

## Now text-grounded (extraction succeeded; documents cite the real text)
- Bessel van der Kolk, *The Body Keeps the Score*: trauma as an imprint on mind, brain, and body; trauma reorganizes perception; agency begins with interoception; self-regulation depends on a friendly relationship with the body; stress hormones do not return to baseline after trauma; developmental trauma. Grounds: stress & the nervous system, mental & emotional wellbeing, supporting knowledge.
- Stan Tatkin & Harville Hendrix, *Wired for Love*: the anchor/island/wave attachment framing; the couple bubble (partners as each other's primary go-to people for safety and security). Grounds: relational intelligence.
- *Somatic Psychotherapy Toolbox*: grounding through the body, orienting movements, shaking/discharge, self-resourcing, the corrected understanding of breath (the long exhale, not forced deep breathing), the somatic inventory of burnout. Grounds: stress & the nervous system.
- *Toward an Integrative Somatic Depth Psychotherapeutic Model for Relational Trauma*: the NeuroAffective Relational Model (NARM); relational trauma stored as implicit, body-level memory; repair through somatic, emotional, and relational processes. Grounds: stress & the nervous system, relational intelligence.
- *2015 Integrative Psychology* text: consciousness as a product of integrative activity; the whole-organism view. Grounds (lightly): mental & emotional wellbeing / the integration premise. (Older, more philosophical source; modern grounding via Siegel carries the spine.)
