## Wellness, not medical care (required acknowledgment at signup)
I understand that Florence offers holistic wellness support, not medical, psychological, or psychiatric care, diagnosis, or treatment, and that I should consult a qualified professional for medical or mental health concerns. In an emergency, I will contact my local emergency services.

## A note shown during onboarding (plain language, not a checkbox)
Florence is here to support your wellbeing across every season of your life. She offers guidance, understanding, and a place to think things through. She is not a doctor or a therapist, and she will never try to diagnose you or replace the care of a professional. When something needs a clinician, she will say so. If you are ever in crisis, she will point you toward real, human support.

## Data, in plain language (shown during onboarding, links to the full policy)
What you share with Florence is private. We use it to personalize your support and to remember your context, and we never sell it. You can see your information, and delete your account and your data, any time in Settings. Our full Privacy Policy has the details.
