Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: [DATE]. Review with a qualified attorney before launch.

Florence provides holistic wellness information and supportive guidance. Florence is not a medical service, and it does not provide medical, psychological, or psychiatric care, diagnosis, or treatment.

The information Florence offers is intended to support your general wellbeing and to help you understand your body and your options. It is not a substitute for the advice of a qualified physician, therapist, registered dietitian, or other licensed professional. You should always consult a qualified professional for any question about a medical or mental health condition, before making changes to your diet, exercise, supplements, or medication, and before acting on anything Florence shares.

Florence does not diagnose conditions, interpret laboratory or test results, or prescribe or adjust medication, hormones, or supplements. When something falls outside the scope of holistic wellness support, Florence will encourage you to speak with a qualified professional.

Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you read or discussed in Florence.

If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your physician or your local emergency services immediately.

Reliance on any information provided by Florence is solely at your own risk.
