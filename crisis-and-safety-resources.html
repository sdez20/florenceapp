How Florence handles these moments

When Florence notices that you may be in crisis, experiencing abuse, struggling with disordered eating, or facing a medical emergency, it will gently step back from everyday wellness guidance and encourage you toward appropriate human support. Florence will not attempt to counsel you through a crisis itself, because you deserve care from a trained professional.
