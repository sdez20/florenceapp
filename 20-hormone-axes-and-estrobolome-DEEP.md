# 20 · Hormone Axes and the Estrobolome (DEEP)

**Status:** New file. Sits upstream of the life phase file (22) and downstream of gut health (17).

## 20.1 Why this file exists

Florence currently treats gut health and hormone health as adjacent subjects. They are one system. The estrobolome is the mechanism that joins them, and without it Florence cannot correctly answer the most common question in this category, which is some version of why a woman's digestion and her cycle got worse at the same time.

---

## 20.2 The four axes Florence needs to hold at once

A woman's hormone picture is the product of four systems interacting. Florence should never answer a hormone question by looking at only one.

**The ovarian axis.** Hypothalamus releases GnRH in pulses, pituitary releases FSH and LH, ovary produces estradiol and progesterone. The pulse generator is exquisitely sensitive to energy availability and to perceived threat, which is why undereating, overtraining, and sustained stress all suppress ovulation through the same door. `[VERIFIED]`

**The adrenal axis.** Hypothalamus, pituitary, adrenal cortex, cortisol. Cortisol follows a daily curve: sharp rise in the thirty to forty five minutes after waking, then decline across the day to a midnight trough. Health lives in the shape of that curve rather than in a single value. `[VERIFIED]`

**The thyroid axis.** TSH drives T4 production, and T4 converts peripherally to the active T3. Sustained cortisol elevation impairs that conversion and raises reverse T3, producing hypothyroid symptoms alongside a normal TSH. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**The metabolic axis.** Insulin, glucose, and the tissues that respond to them. Insulin is not only a fuel hormone in women. It directly stimulates ovarian androgen production and suppresses hepatic SHBG synthesis, so it is a sex hormone regulator in its own right. `[VERIFIED]`

### The interaction rules Florence applies

- Sustained cortisol elevation degrades ovulation, thyroid conversion, and insulin sensitivity at the same time. A woman reporting fatigue, cycle change, and weight change together is describing one upstream problem more often than three separate ones. `[DOCUMENTED]`
- Progesterone's metabolite allopregnanolone acts on GABA receptors. When ovulation stops, that calming effect stops with it. This is why anxiety and sleep disruption are frequently the first perimenopausal symptoms rather than hot flushes. `[DOCUMENTED]`
- Thyroid dysfunction, iron deficiency, and perimenopause produce an almost identical symptom set. Florence routes to testing before it routes to self management. See 23.

## 20.3 The estrobolome, in full

### The mechanism

1. Estradiol circulates and acts on tissue.
2. The liver conjugates it, attaching a glucuronide or sulfate group. The molecule becomes water soluble and is marked for excretion.
3. Conjugated estrogen leaves in bile and enters the intestine.
4. In the gut, bacteria producing the enzyme **beta-glucuronidase** cleave the glucuronide group off, returning the estrogen to its free, active form.
5. Free estrogen is reabsorbed across the intestinal wall and re-enters circulation.

The collection of gut bacterial genes capable of metabolising estrogens in this way is the **estrobolome**. `[VERIFIED]` as mechanism.

### The three consequences that matter clinically

- A woman's circulating estrogen exposure depends partly on her microbiome, not only on her ovaries. `[VERIFIED]`
- **Transit time is a hormone variable.** Slow transit gives beta-glucuronidase longer to act on conjugated estrogen sitting in the colon. Constipation is a hormonal matter as well as a digestive one. `[DOCUMENTED]`
- Reduced microbial diversity shifts estrobolome composition, which is why the dietary levers that build diversity keep appearing in estrogen related conditions. `[DOCUMENTED]`

### What Florence must not overclaim

There is no validated beta-glucuronidase threshold that tells a woman she has excessive estrogen recirculation. Links to fibroids, endometriosis, and breast cancer risk are mechanistically coherent with supporting associative data, and are not proven causal chains. `[EMERGING]` Florence teaches the mechanism with confidence and marks the clinical claims as still developing.

## 20.4 The levers, ordered by strength

1. **Fibre.** Binds conjugated estrogen, speeds transit, feeds diversity. The most reliable lever available. `[VERIFIED]`
2. **Plant diversity across the week.** The number of distinct plants predicts microbial diversity more strongly than the quantity of any one. Herbs, spices, nuts, seeds, beans, and whole grains all count. `[DOCUMENTED]`
3. **Cruciferous and bitter greens.** Glucosinolates yield sulforaphane and indole-3-carbinol, which shift liver estrogen metabolism toward less proliferative metabolite ratios. `[DOCUMENTED]` for the metabolite shift, `[EMERGING]` for downstream clinical benefit.
4. **Transit.** A daily, complete, unstrained bowel movement. Frequently a nervous system question before it is a fibre question, see 20.5.
5. **Calcium D-glucarate.** Marketed as a beta-glucuronidase inhibitor. `[EMERGING]` Florence does not build guidance around it.

## 20.5 Why fibre sometimes makes things worse

Motility runs on parasympathetic activity. A body held in sympathetic tone does not prioritise moving food through the colon. Add habitual pelvic floor holding and a woman can eat forty grams of fibre a day and still not go. `[DOCUMENTED]`

**Order of operations Florence follows:** nervous system, then hydration and transit, then fibre increases introduced slowly. Adding fibre first to a body in sympathetic tone produces bloating and discouragement, and the woman concludes that holistic approaches do not work for her.

Tools with real support:
- **Cyclic sighing.** Two inhales through the nose, the second short and topping up the lungs, then a long slow exhale through the mouth. Five minutes daily. Outperformed mindfulness meditation for mood and reduced respiratory rate in a controlled trial. `[VERIFIED]`
- Any breathing pattern with the exhale longer than the inhale raises parasympathetic activity. `[VERIFIED]`
- Position: knees above hips changes the anorectal angle and reduces straining. `[VERIFIED]`
- Magnesium citrate draws water into the bowel. Magnesium glycinate is better tolerated for sleep and calm. Different forms, different jobs. `[DOCUMENTED]`
- Morning light within an hour of waking, dim light in the evening, to anchor the cortisol curve at the correct hour. `[VERIFIED]`

## 20.6 Cultural layer

Every foodway already solved fibre and fermentation. Florence reaches for what a woman has in her kitchen before it reaches for a supplement or for produce that is unfamiliar and expensive to her.

**Caribbean.** Callaloo, okra, green banana, breadfruit, pigeon peas, red peas, bitter melon, sorrel, dasheen leaf.
**West African.** Bitter leaf, ogi and other fermented grain porridges, garri, egusi, okra soup, black eyed peas, moringa, ugu.
**South Asian.** Dal in every form, methi, mustard greens, idli and dosa batter, curd, karela, jackfruit, drumstick.
**Latin American.** Frijoles, nopales, chayote, quelites, plantain, fermented atoles, hibiscus, epazote.
**East and Southeast Asian.** Kimchi, miso, natto, gai lan, choy sum, burdock, seaweed, tempeh.
**East African.** Injera from fermented teff batter, gomen, shiro, lentils, ayib.
**Middle Eastern and North African.** Lentils, chickpeas, mloukhieh, freekeh, labneh, preserved lemon, fresh herb quantities used as vegetables rather than garnish.

Mustard greens, methi, gai lan, choy sum, bitter leaf, and amaranth style callaloo carry the same bitter and glucosinolate families as the broccoli and kale that every wellness article names. **Florence never implies a woman must adopt foreign food to get a hormonal benefit.** Doing so is the exact experience that made holistic care feel like it belongs to someone else.

## 20.7 Response guidance

When a woman describes cycle symptoms and digestive symptoms together, Florence names the connection explicitly and simply. Something close to: your digestion and your cycle are running through the same system, and the way your body clears estrogen depends partly on how well things are moving through your gut. Then it offers the nervous system step before the food step.

Florence does not present the estrobolome as a diagnosis, does not suggest a woman has an estrogen clearance problem, and does not recommend testing to prove it.

---

## 20.8 Never

- Never tell a woman she has estrogen dominance. It is not a diagnostic category with agreed criteria.
- Never recommend a supplement to inhibit beta-glucuronidase.
- Never give fibre targets, plant counts, or cruciferous guidance once the disordered eating flag has fired. See 23 and the file 13 supplement.
- Never suggest a woman stop or change a prescribed medication to improve her gut or hormone picture.
